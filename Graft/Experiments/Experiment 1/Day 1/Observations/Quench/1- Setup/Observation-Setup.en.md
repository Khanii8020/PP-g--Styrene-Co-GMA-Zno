# Technical Description of Visual Observations: Quench / 1- Setup

## 1. Scope of This Record

This file documents only the apparatus arrangement and initial charge recorded in `Quench/1- Setup` for Experiment 1, Day 1. It is based on the `README.docx` located in this folder and the two photographs, `ستاپ 1.jpg` and `ستاپ 2.jpg`.

This description does not cover the subsequent heating process, complete dissolution of polypropylene, quenching, separation, or sediment collection. Those stages are documented in separate observation folders.

## 2. General Arrangement

The documented system consists of a nominal 250 mL four-neck glass reaction flask, a vertically installed coiled condenser, a paraffin-oil bath in a large beaker, and a heating/magnetic-stirring assembly. The reaction flask is positioned in the paraffin bath, while the condenser extends upward from the flask.

### 2.1. Reaction Flask and Connections

- Reaction vessel: four-neck glass flask.
- Nominal flask capacity: 250 mL.
- Three flask necks are closed with stoppers.
- The main neck is connected to the condenser through a glass adapter.
- According to the README, the initial flask charge consists of 10 mL of xylene and 2 g of polypropylene granules.

### 2.2. Condenser and Cooling-Water Circuit

- The condenser is described as a coiled/spiral condenser.
- The approximate length of the coiled section is recorded in the README as 30 to 40 cm.
- The README states that cold water is circulated through the condenser.
- The glass coil and connected hoses can be seen in the photographs.
- The water inlet/outlet orientation, flow rate, and water temperature are not specified in the README and cannot be determined reliably from the photographs.

### 2.3. Paraffin Bath and Heating Unit

- The bath vessel is a beaker with a nominal capacity of 2000 mL.
- The bath liquid is paraffin, with a recorded volume of 600 mL.
- The beaker is placed on a hotplate magnetic stirrer.
- The reaction flask is positioned in the bath so that heat is transferred to the reaction vessel through the bath.
- In the overview photograph, the bath beaker is visible on the heating/stirring plate, within the arrangement of support stands and clamps.

The recorded 600 mL is the volume of paraffin in the bath. It must not be confused with the volume of xylene charged into the reaction flask.

### 2.4. Stirring

The README records the following stirring details:

- Magnetic stirring is used.
- The recorded magnet size is 2 mm.
- The stirring speed is 500 revolutions per minute (rpm).
- The stated purpose of this speed is to prevent xylene splashing, formation of a strong vortex, and impact of PP granules against the flask wall.

The README does not clarify whether the 2 mm value refers to the stir bar's length, diameter, or nominal size. The value is therefore reproduced as recorded, without further interpretation.

## 3. Initial Flask Charge

The quantities recorded in this folder are:

| Component or setting | Recorded value |
|---|---:|
| Polypropylene (PP) | 2 g |
| Xylene | 10 mL |
| Xylene-to-PP ratio | 5 mL per g |
| Paraffin in the bath | 600 mL |
| Nominal bath-beaker capacity | 2000 mL |
| Nominal reaction-flask capacity | 250 mL |
| Stirring speed | 500 rpm |
| Magnet size, as stated in README | 2 mm |

The xylene-to-polypropylene ratio calculated from the recorded quantities is:

[
\frac{10\ \mathrm{mL\ xylene}}{2\ \mathrm{g\ PP}}=5\ \mathrm{mL/g}
]

The README states that the referenced articles use 5 mL of xylene per 1 g of PP. Accordingly, the initial charge in this folder, equivalent to 5 mL/g, is numerically consistent with the literature ratio cited in the README. The cited articles have not been independently reassessed in this folder-specific description; this statement reflects the comparison already made in the README.

## 7. Technical Summary

In the `1- Setup` stage, a 250 mL four-neck flask is connected to a coiled condenser and arranged in a paraffin bath. The bath consists of 600 mL of paraffin in a 2000 mL beaker, placed on a hotplate magnetic stirrer. According to the README, magnetic stirring is performed using a magnet recorded as 2 mm in size at 500 rpm.

The initial flask charge is 2 g of PP and 10 mL of xylene, corresponding to a xylene-to-PP ratio of 5 mL/g. The first photograph records the overall apparatus arrangement, while the second provides a closer view of the lower assembly during the initial minutes. The README states that the PP granules had not yet dissolved at the time of the second photograph.

This summary is limited to the apparatus setup and initial charge documented in `1- Setup`. It does not establish that any subsequent experimental stage was completed or successful.
